# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
static HTML/CSS/JS

## Users
Institutional or professional DeFi investors looking for automated RWA yields.

## Product Purpose
OmniRWA aggregates tokenized sovereign debt, commercial real estate loans, and green infrastructure into an ERC-4626 vault dynamically rebalanced by on-chain AI agents on BOT Chain.

## Positioning
Dynamic rebalancing of tokenized sovereign debt, real estate, and green infra by on-chain AI agents on BOT Chain.

## Operating Context
Web3 dapp dashboard served locally, displaying RWA strategies, live AI telemetry, restaking tiers, and yield calculations.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- ERC-4626 OmniVault deposit/withdraw interactions.
- Restaking tier selection and simulation.
- Real-time AI Agent status indicator.
- Proof of Reserve / Attestation verification.
- Local storage and mock contract interactions using ethers.js.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: OmniRWA
- Color Palette: Institutional Matte (Dark mode with #00D492 accent, Light mode Swiss-style).
- Voice: Professional, institutional, high-contrast, clean UI structure.
