Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding tokenized real-world assets, AI rebalancing, and restaking.

Tokenized RWAs represent real financial contracts—such as US Treasuries, commercial real estate loans, and green energy infrastructure—brought on-chain as ERC-20 tokens. OmniRWA pools these yield-bearing assets into an ERC-4626 vault so users can deposit standard stablecoins (USDT) and earn genuine real-world yield.

The AI Strategy Agent (powered by Gemini 3.7 Flash) analyzes off-chain macroeconomic indicators, interest rates, and counterparty risks. When market conditions shift, the AI computes optimal portfolio weights and dispatches verifiable on-chain rebalancing transactions through the AiStrategyController contract on BOT Chain.

When you deposit stablecoins into OmniVault, you receive omniRWA vault shares. You can restake these shares into our restaking contract across 4 lock tiers (Flexible to 180 Days) to lock collateral and earn up to a 2.0x points multiplier + native BOT reward points.

All underlying collateral assets are verified via cryptographic IPFS custody attestations. Each RWA strategy contract (bUSTB, bREIT, bGREEN) links directly to institutional custodians, legal title documents, and Net Asset Value (NAV) audit reports on the BOT Chain explorer.
